The Lounge

Good vibes, on purpose.

We built Urban Puff around the simple things that make a night worth staying out for: a hookah that actually pulls right, food that comes out hot, a drink that doesn’t need alcohol to be interesting, and seats you don’t want to get up from.

Whether it’s a birthday table of fifteen, a game night with the guys, or a slow Tuesday with one friend and one bowl — the room makes space for it.

Premium Hookah

Top-shelf shisha, clean heat management and a flavour list that runs from classic mint to house-blend mixes. Set up fresh, tended all night.

Comfortable Seating

Deep lounge sofas, low tables and booths made for long sessions with a big group or a quiet two.

Free Wi-Fi

Fast, free and open to every guest — work late, stream, or just keep the group chat going.

Great Music

Afrobeats, hip-hop, R&B and Desi hits — the playlist moves with the room, and the volume stays talk-friendly.

Sports on Screen

Big screens on the wall for the game — football, cricket, basketball, the fights. Come early for a good seat.

Kitchen Till Late

Tikka, kababs, mains and sides coming out of the kitchen long after most places have closed.

From the Kitchen & Bar

Eat well. Sip well. Stay late.

Four things we care about more than we probably should.

The Bowl

Premium Hookah

House mixes and single-origin shisha, packed fresh and coal-managed all night so the last pull is as good as the first.

The Grill

Tikka & Kababs

Marinated overnight, skewered to order and charred hard over open heat. The smell hits the room before the plate does.

The Bar

Mocktails

Zero proof, full flavour. Fresh fruit, real citrus and a proper shake — built to stand up to the grill.

The Finish

Sweet Endings

Gulab jamun, kulfi and something chocolate for the last hour of the night, when nobody actually wants to leave.
