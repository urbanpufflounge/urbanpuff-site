Work From the Lounge

A laptop-friendly lounge in Marietta, GA — open from 1 PM.

Swap the kitchen table for a lounge sofa. Free Wi-Fi, comfortable seating, a full kitchen and a bar that runs from Red Bull to craft cocktails — the remote workday, done better.

Remote Work in Marietta

Your afternoon office, on Wylie Rd SE.

Most hookah lounges don’t open until the evening. Urban Puff opens at 1:00 PM every day, which makes us one of the few places in Marietta where you can work remotely in the afternoon and stay on for the night.

Whether you’re a remote employee tired of the home office, a freelancer between clients, or a student from Kennesaw State or Life University with a deadline, the afternoon room is yours: free Wi-Fi, a comfortable seat, food when you need it and a hookah when you’ve earned it.

Remote Work FAQ

Working from Urban Puff.

Can I work remotely from Urban Puff?

Yes. We open at 1:00 PM every day, the Wi-Fi is free, and the sofas and booths are built for long sessions. Bring the laptop, order something from the kitchen or the bar, and settle in for the afternoon.

Is the Wi-Fi free?

Free for every guest. Ask the floor for the password when you sit down.

What time do you open?

1:00 PM every day. We stay open till 1:00 AM Sunday to Thursday and 3:00 AM on Friday and Saturday, so a workday can roll straight into the evening.

Is it quiet enough for calls?

Afternoons are the calmest part of our day, and the music stays at a talk-friendly volume. For a long video call, grab a booth toward the back.

Can I bring my team?

Yes — off-site meetings, team lunches and after-work sessions are welcome. Reserve a table so we can hold the space for your group.

Do I need to be 21?

Yes. Urban Puff is a hookah lounge and bar, so every guest needs to be 21+ with a valid photo ID, day or night.
